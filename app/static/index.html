FAQ

Before you press generate.

When will paid billing open?＋

After the founder beta confirms generation costs and performance at real usage levels. Free accounts are available now.

Do failed jobs use credits?＋

No. Credits reserved for a failed generation are returned automatically.

Can I clone any voice?＋

No. You must own the voice or have the speaker’s explicit permission. The app requires that attestation before cloning.

What happens to my reference audio?＋

Reference samples are used for the requested generation and deleted afterward.

Can I cancel a paid plan?＋

Yes. Paid plans will be month-to-month when billing opens.

Is there an enterprise plan?＋

Yes. Contact us for custom volume, deployment and support requirements.

We collect account details, generation settings, scripts, job records and technical logs needed to operate AI Voice HQ. Voice reference files are processed only for the requested generation.

We use this information to provide the service, secure accounts, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and improve product reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Reference audio used for voice cloning is deleted after generation. Finished audio and job history remain available to the signed-in account until removed under our retention process.
