PRODUCT GUIDE

Everything to know before you generate.

Clear answers about voices, languages, credits, privacy, downloads and founder pricing.

How do credits work?＋

One source-script character costs one credit for each generated output. A 1,000-character script costs 1,000 credits for one take, 2,000 credits for two takes, or 3,000 credits when rendered into three languages. Spaces and punctuation count because they are part of the text the model processes. The current 5,000-credit beta allowance is issued once when the account is created; it does not reset monthly and does not expire during the founder beta.

What happens when my free credits run out?＋

Generation pauses when the balance reaches zero. You can still sign in and access completed work in private history, but you cannot start a new generation until additional beta credits are granted or paid plans and top-ups open. AI Voice HQ is not accepting payments yet.

Which voices can I use without recording anything?＋

The studio currently includes 29 ready voices covering narration, explainers, character work and several accents and styles. Choose a voice from the catalogue and generate immediately. Recording or uploading audio is only required when you deliberately select the authorized voice-cloning option.

Which output languages are supported?＋

Multilingual generation currently supports Arabic, Danish, German, Greek, English, Spanish, Finnish, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Dutch, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Swedish, Swahili, Turkish and Chinese. Availability can also depend on the selected voice and model.

How does the translation workflow work?＋

Paste one source script, enable translation, let the studio detect the source language or choose it yourself, then select up to five output languages. The studio translates the text and creates a separate voiceover for every selected language. Each output uses credits based on the original source-script length.

Can I clone any person’s voice?＋

No. You may only clone your own voice or a speaker who has given you explicit permission. The studio requires a consent attestation before cloning. Non-consensual impersonation, fraud, deception and misuse of another person’s identity are prohibited.

What makes a good voice-cloning reference?＋

Use a clean 6–30 second recording containing one speaker, natural speech and as little music, echo or background noise as possible. The studio accepts WAV, MP3, M4A and WebM uploads, and you can also record a sample in the browser. A clearer reference usually produces a more consistent result.

What happens to my reference audio?＋

A one-time reference is normalized for the requested generation and deleted when that job finishes, including when it fails. If you explicitly choose Save to My Voices, the normalized sample and its consent record are stored privately so you can reuse it across projects and languages. Only your signed-in account can list, preview, rename, replace or delete it, and deleting it removes the stored sample.

Can I save and reuse my own voice?＋

Yes. Select Create a new voice, add a clean 6–30 second sample, confirm the speaker’s permission, switch on Save to My Voices and give it a recognizable name. You can set an accent, primary language, use-case tags and default delivery. Saved voices appear at the top of the Studio voice picker and can be managed from your profile.

What can I download, and where do I find past work?＋

Completed generations are delivered as WAV audio. Open History to replay or download previous jobs, individual candidate takes and each language output. History is tied to the signed-in account so another visitor cannot browse your generations.

Do failed jobs use credits?＋

No. Credits are reserved when a job starts so two jobs cannot spend the same balance. If generation fails, the reserved amount is returned automatically. Successful jobs use the displayed estimate, including additional takes or language outputs.

How long can a script be?＋

The current editor accepts up to 5,000 characters per generation. For longer projects, divide the script at natural section or paragraph breaks. This also makes it easier to compare takes, correct pronunciation and regenerate only the part that needs revision.

Can I publish or use the generated audio commercially?＋

You may use generated audio in projects you have the right to publish. You remain responsible for the script, trademarks, reference recordings, speaker permissions and any other source material you provide. Do not use the service to impersonate, mislead, defraud or violate another person’s rights.

How are paid subscriptions, renewals and cancellations handled?＋

Paid billing uses Paddle as the merchant of record. Paddle securely processes payment details, calculates applicable taxes, provides invoices and manages renewals. Monthly plans add the displayed allowance after each successful monthly payment. Annual plans are paid once and issue the full annual allowance immediately. Failed or incomplete payments never add credits. You can open the hosted billing portal from the studio to update payment details, view invoices or cancel; cancellation stops the next renewal while existing access continues through the paid period.

Is there a plan for agencies or high-volume teams?＋

Yes. The Studio tier is designed for recurring production work, and custom arrangements can cover larger volumes, deployment requirements and direct support. Email hello@aivoicegeneratorhq.com with expected monthly usage, languages, team size and workflow requirements.

LEGAL

Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

What we collect

We collect account details, generation settings, scripts, job records and technical logs needed to operate AI Voice HQ. Voice reference files are processed only for the requested generation unless you explicitly save one to My Voices. For paid accounts, we store the Paddle customer, subscription and transaction identifiers needed to provide credits and account support.

Payments

Paddle is our merchant of record and processes card details, billing addresses, taxes, invoices, renewals and refunds. AI Voice HQ does not receive or store complete card numbers or card security codes.

How we use information

We use this information to provide the service, secure accounts, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and improve product reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Voice reference files

One-time reference audio is deleted after generation. A reference you deliberately save to My Voices is stored in private audio storage with its consent attestation and remains available only to your signed-in account until you replace or delete it. Finished audio and job history remain available to the signed-in account under our current retention process.

Service providers

We use infrastructure providers for authentication, databases, GPU processing, file storage and subscription billing. They process data only to provide those services.

Your choices

You can delete a saved voice and its stored sample directly from My Voices. You may request account or other stored-data deletion by emailing hello@aivoicegeneratorhq.com.
